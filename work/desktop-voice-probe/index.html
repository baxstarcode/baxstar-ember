  1. Talk → review the final words → Send transcript → Play response. Check the actual mic indicator turns off and the reply is audible. Record the result below.

  4. While listening, switch apps or tabs briefly and return. On iPhone, also try locking/unlocking once. Listening and playback must not restart on their own.
